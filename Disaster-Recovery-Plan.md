# Disaster Recovery Plan

## Introduction

A Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) is a documented process that helps organizations restore systems, data, and operations after a major disruption.

A proper recovery plan reduces downtime and helps maintain business continuity.

---

# 1. Purpose of Disaster Recovery

A disaster recovery plan helps to:

- Restore important systems
- Recover lost data
- Reduce downtime
- Protect critical resources
- Maintain continuous operations

---

# 2. Common Disaster Scenarios

Examples:

## Hardware Failure

Problems with:

- Servers
- Storage devices
- Network equipment

---

## Cybersecurity Incidents

Examples:

- Malware attacks
- Ransomware
- Unauthorized access

---

## Natural Disasters

Examples:

- Fire
- Flood
- Earthquake
- Severe weather

---

## Human Errors

Examples:

- Accidental deletion
- Incorrect configuration
- System changes

---

# 3. Disaster Recovery Process

## Step 1: Preparation

Prepare recovery resources:

- Backup systems
- Recovery procedures
- Emergency contacts
- Documentation

---

## Step 2: Identification

Determine:

- What happened
- Affected systems
- Level of damage

---

## Step 3: Containment

Limit the impact:

- Isolate affected systems
- Prevent further damage
- Protect remaining resources

---

## Step 4: Recovery

Restore operations by:

- Recovering backups
- Repairing systems
- Testing functionality

---

## Step 5: Review

After recovery:

- Document the incident
- Identify improvements
- Update recovery plans

---

# 4. Backup and Recovery Strategy

A good strategy includes:

- Regular backups
- Secure backup storage
- Recovery testing
- Backup verification

---

# 5. Recovery Priorities

Critical systems should be restored first:

1. Network infrastructure
2. Authentication services
3. Business applications
4. Databases
5. User devices

---

# 6. Recovery Team Responsibilities

Roles may include:

## IT Team

Responsible for:

- System recovery
- Network restoration
- Technical troubleshooting

---

## Management Team

Responsible for:

- Decision making
- Communication
- Resource allocation

---

# 7. Disaster Recovery Best Practices

Follow these practices:

- Maintain updated documentation
- Test recovery procedures regularly
- Keep backups secure
- Train recovery personnel
- Review plans after incidents

---

# 8. Disaster Recovery Checklist

- [ ] Recovery plan documented
- [ ] Backups available
- [ ] Recovery steps tested
- [ ] Emergency contacts updated
- [ ] Critical systems identified
- [ ] Recovery responsibilities assigned

---

# Conclusion

A Disaster Recovery Plan helps organizations recover quickly from unexpected events.

Proper preparation, secure backups, and regular testing are essential for maintaining system availability and reducing downtime.
